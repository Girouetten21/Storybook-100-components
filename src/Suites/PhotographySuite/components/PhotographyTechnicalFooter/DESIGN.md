# Photography Technical Footer // The Final Narrative Exhibit

## Overview
A technical, metadata-rich footer designed to close the 'Photography Suite' portfolio with authority and aesthetic precision. It blends the brutalalism of camera hardware with the elegance of luxury editorial design.

## Visual Identity
- **Grand Title Exhibition**: A massive, italicized title ("THE ART OF LIGHT") that serves as the visual anchor for the site's closure.
- **Metadata Ribbon**: A bottom-aligned telemetry strip detailing studio-standard specs like Focal Length, White Point (D65), and Year of Establishment.
- **Technical Navigation**: Links are organized under JETBRAINS MONO headers with high-contrast states and subtle magnetic-like padding transitions.
- **Quiet Luxury Palette**: Uses a pure black background (`#000`) with golden-bronze accents (`#d4af37`) for a premium studio feel.

## Tech Stack
- **GSAP ScrollTrigger**: Orchestrates the staggered reveal of the massive title and columns as they enter the viewport.
- **CSS Clamp**: Ensures the massive typography scales perfectly across mobile and ultra-wide displays.
- **Semantic HTML**: Uses `<footer>`, `<ul>`, and `<address>` elements for SEO and accessibility.

## Usage
```tsx
<PhotographyTechnicalFooter />
```
